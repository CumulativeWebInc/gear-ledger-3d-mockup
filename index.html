What the colors mean

🟢Green pavilion glow — department pavilion (DISTRIBUTION island). One per CWI department.
🟡Gold monument — most-traveled gear pedestal (LEGENDS island). Taller glow = more handoffs (SAMPLE scale).
🔵Blue podium tower — overall leaderboard rank (LEADERBOARDS island). Height = rank position.
🟣Purple arc — gear currently in transit (HANDOFFS island). Animated dash = live-sample movement.
⚪White walkway plaque — one handoff record in chronological order (CHAIN island).
🌉Bridge — a handoff relationship between two islands' domains.

All colors describe mockup semantics. All underlying figures are SAMPLE data.

This 3D archipelago is a design concept for the CWI Gear Ledger. All figures are illustrative sample data, not live ledger values. Departments, agents, gear items, counts, routes, and timelines shown here are invented for the mockup and do not represent real CWI operations.

In the live product, each figure would link to a signed, verifiable handoff record. Here, "View evidence" explains what that link would do.
